# Landing page strategy

Establish the audience, offer, one primary action, traffic source, likely objections, available proof and actual terms. Infer what the brief settles and state material assumptions. Let the page length and structure follow what the visitor needs to decide.

Make the opening communicate the outcome, the offer and a clear action. Match the promise to the traffic source. Explain the benefits and how the offer works, put real proof beside the relevant claim, and address objections where they arise. Repeat the same primary action when useful. A high-intent campaign may need only a short page; an unfamiliar offer may need a longer argument. Avoid competing primary actions and vague claims.

Use supplied facts and assets. Never invent metrics, testimonials, logos, guarantees or trial terms. A concrete placeholder can illustrate copy, but must not masquerade as evidence.
