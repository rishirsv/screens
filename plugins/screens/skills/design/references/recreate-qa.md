# Recreation and design QA

For a recreation, inspect the actual reference and identify its viewport, content, major geometry, type, colors, imagery and distinctive details. Preserve the reference and its meaningful content. Work from observed evidence, not an imagined version; state what is inaccessible. Recreate with editable HTML pieces, keeping assets in the bundle and using locally available fonts with similar metrics when the original is unavailable.

For QA, compare the accepted target and current poster at the same viewport and scale. First correct structure and major proportions, then typography, spacing, color and details. Use `read_object` to inspect exact source values and target the relevant piece. Check long text, clipping and any required behavior separately. Limit correction to two rounds, then name remaining differences and anything not inspected; do not claim pixel fidelity or working interactions from a poster alone.
